AI-Based Irrigation Advisory System

This system uses AI to provide smart irrigation recommendations to farmers.

Irrigation Recommendation

Water the crop only when the soil moisture is low.

Recommended irrigation: 25 m3 of water per acre.

Use water carefully and avoid unnecessary irrigation to conserve water.

Measurements

Water contains H2O and a small volume can be measured in cm3.

Recommended temperature: 25°C

Agricultural Technologies

IoT sensors collect farm data. GIS helps analyze field locations. GPS identifies farm locations. ET helps estimate crop water requirements.

Advisory note: Recommendations may change according to soil, weather, crop type, and field conditions.
